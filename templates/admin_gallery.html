Photo checklist: what to photograph

The stairs (the way up) · the entrance · the counter while coffee is made · 3 to 4 close-ups of the signature dishes · a full table in the evening light · each cosy corner of the interior · the team at work (with their permission) · the view. Shoot with the phone held level, in daylight if possible, and keep the photos at least 1600 pixels wide.
